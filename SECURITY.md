# Security

Uplink holds Tesla sign-in tokens for its owners, so security reports matter a lot to us.

## Reporting a problem

Please don't open a public issue. Instead, either:

- use GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/gokulkrishh/getuplink.in/security/advisories/new), or
- email getuplink.app@gmail.com.

Say what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it. We'll reply within a few days and keep you posted until it's fixed.

## In scope

- The app at getuplink.in and app.getuplink.in, and the code in this repository.
- Sign-in, sessions, invites, sharing links, the `/api/mcp` connector and the cron routes.

Please test only against your own account or your own copy of Uplink. Don't read, change or delete anyone else's data, and don't run load or denial-of-service tests.
